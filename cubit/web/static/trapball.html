How to play

1. Choose Horizontal or Vertical wall (H / V keys).
2. Click the playfield - a wall grows both ways.
3. If a ball hits the incomplete wall, you lose a life.
4. Finished walls split the room. Empty regions fill in.
5. Claim the quota to clear the level.
